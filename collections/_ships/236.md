The Japanese Myoko and Takao classes are amongst my favourites of all WW2 cruisers. They were heavily armed, fast and imposing-looking vessels. In 2006 I decided to build Hasegawa's newly re-tooled (2000) kit of Myoko. My plan was to build a highly-detailed and accurate model to displace from the display shelf my old model of the cruiser Chikuma (M58) which I had built in the 1980s. My plan was thwarted by the difficulty posed by the Flyhawk dedicated detail set which proved to be far beyond my patience and skill level. As a result, the project languished in the &quot;too-hard-basket&quot; for many years. In 2024 I decided to finish it and accept compromises in accuracy and scale fidelity. I did use some of the troublesome Flyhawk parts, and added many other aftermarket items to eventually finish the model 18 years after starting it.
